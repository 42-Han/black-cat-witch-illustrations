# Brand DNA

## Visual sentence

Soft retro knowledge illustration: a cute pixel black cat witch inside a grey, blue, and pink hand-drawn ink/marker workbench. The image should feel calm, clever, slightly whimsical, clear, and easy to reuse across Chinese AI tutorials.

## Color tokens

Use these tokens as the default palette:

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Canvas | `#F8F7F3` | warm-white background, used sparingly so it does not read as yellow |
| Pale blue | `#DCEBFF` | quiet fill, soft atmosphere, secondary area |
| Deep blue | `#294A91` | main structure, header strip, key state, primary flow |
| Pale pink | `#FFE0EC` | gentle highlight, secondary region |
| Dusty pink | `#F08AB7` | important signal, result, small accent, character detail |
| Frame grey | `#C6C8CD` | window or workbench structure |
| Shadow grey | `#5B606A` | restrained structural shadow and depth line |
| Ink | `#1E2635` | cat, outlines, symbols, short labels |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | clean reading surface and negative space |

Keep grey, blue, and pink visually dominant. Use pink and blue as independent signals; do not blend them into a rainbow or neon gradient. Avoid dominant yellow, gold, orange, tan, warm beige, and sunny lighting. A faint warm-white canvas is acceptable; if the image still looks yellow, cool the canvas toward white and reduce paper warmth.

## Material language

- Pixel character: chunky, limited-palette pixel clusters, readable at thumbnail size, rounded silhouette rather than sharp game combat geometry.
- Environment: clear hand-drawn ink, felt-tip, or marker strokes with slightly organic line variation; simple flat forms; crisp readable edges; restrained paper texture; no glossy 3D rendering.
- Retro structure: one grey window or workbench shell at most, with a deep-blue header strip if it helps organize the single mechanism.
- Background: quiet cool-white paper or pale-blue field. Keep texture low contrast and away from the main explanation.

## Layout rules

- Default canvas: 16:9 landscape.
- Use internal margins of roughly 6–8% of the canvas.
- Keep at least one calm negative-space area for later Chinese labels or cropping.
- Use one main mechanism only. Do not stack multiple windows, nested cards, dense dashboard controls, or decorative labels.
