# Black Cat Witch IP

## Identity

The recurring character is a small black cat witch who explains difficult AI ideas through simple magical actions. She is clever, calm, slightly absent-minded, and warm without becoming a mascot or a children's cartoon character.

## Stable visual invariants

- Large rounded black cat head and small body.
- Rounded triangular ears; keep them soft rather than needle-sharp.
- Large powder-pink or pale-blue eyes with dark vertical pupils. Never default to yellow or gold.
- Charcoal-black fur and a deep-blue cloak or collar.
- One small pink-blue circular brooch or magic mark as a recurring identifier.
- Tiny rounded paws and a blunt, readable silhouette.
- Pixel-art character in hybrid mode; full pixel treatment only when explicitly requested.

Do not change fur color, eye family, silhouette, proportions, or the pixel-character versus hand-drawn-environment separation without explicit user direction.

## Expression and action

Use curious, focused, mildly surprised, quietly pleased, sleepy, or gently confused expressions. Keep the emotional range readable but restrained.

The cat must be doing the explanation: sorting, connecting, compressing, opening, weighing, catching, repairing, comparing, or guiding one object or signal. Do not place her beside a diagram as a passive observer.

## Props

No default witch hat, wand, laptop, spellbook, crystal, potion bottle, or floating UI cards. Add one or two props only when they make the current idea easier to see. Prefer abstract symbols, cards, a simple workbench, a single window, or one low-tech object.

## Hard avoids

- Sharp claws, aggressive spikes, horror eyes, or threatening magic.
- Yellow, gold, orange, tan, or dominant warm lighting.
- Photorealism, glossy 3D, anime redesign, smooth vector mascot, or detailed game UI.
- Excessive cuteness, baby proportions, sticker behavior, or a generic black-cat icon.
- Costume drift between images.
